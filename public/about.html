About me

Hi, I’m Daisy, a Product Designer and First Class Computer Science graduate from Babcock University.

I enjoy understanding people’s needs, finding the pain points in an experience, and turning those insights into practical design decisions. My work brings together research, interaction design, prototyping, and UI design.

I care about interfaces that are reliable and accessible, with functionality and user needs guiding the visual choices. My computer science background also helps me think through how an experience can be built.
